How it works (30 seconds)

  1. Learn: badged thumbnails shown to a local vision model — PICK / SCROLL / STOP verdicts. Selectors land in a per-site pack.
  2. Replay blind: learned href + TEXT gate (Jaccard ≥0.95) with dHash second opinion. Drift → re-learn flag, one snapshot, stop.
  3. Report: every run writes times, bytes, strikes, vision verdict, token log.

Upkeep, not software

FastBrowse is open source. What we sell is the boring part done right: version pinning, monthly selector re-validation across your site packs, and a healthcheck your team can run. One page, one number: your Anthropic bill, down.
